Cornish Bins – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Cornish Bins is an unofficial Alexa skill that tells you your bin collection days and can remind you the evening before. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cornwall Council. Collection dates come from the council's public website.

What the skill uses, and why

InformationWhyKept?
Your Alexa device's address (only with your permission) To check you are in Cornwall Council's area and find your property on the council's website. No. It is read each time you use the skill and not stored.
Your property reference number (UPRN), from the council's website To look up your collection dates and keep your reminders up to date. Yes, only if you set up reminders.
Your chosen reminder time To set reminders at the time you asked for. Yes, only if you set up reminders.
A list of the anonymous IDs of people who have reminders turned on So reminders can be checked against the council's dates each night, for example when collections move over Christmas. Yes, until you cancel your reminders or disable the skill.
Reminders (only with your permission) To remind you the evening before each collection. Stored by Amazon in your Alexa account, like any other reminder.

Stored items are linked to an anonymous ID that Amazon gives the skill, not to your name, email or Amazon account details, which the skill never receives.

Who it is shared with

Your postcode and property reference are sent to cornwall.gov.uk to look up your addresses and collection dates. Nothing is sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else. The skill runs on Amazon's Alexa-hosted service, so stored data is held by Amazon Web Services.

Deleting your data

  • Say "Alexa, ask Cornish Bins to cancel my bin reminders" to remove your reminders and stop reminder updates.
  • Disable the skill in the Alexa app to stop it using your address and reminders. You can turn off either permission at any time in the skill's settings in the Alexa app.
  • To have your stored property reference and reminder time deleted completely, ask using the contact link below.

Children

The skill is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
